Your first visit

Visit & directions

A little guidance for the last few steps to the silverball.

48 Bridge St, Unit 3A
Nashua, New Hampshire

Find us on the third floor. Stairs and an accessible route via the ramp and elevator are available.

Apple MapsGoogle Maps

Members have 24/7 access. Visiting as a guest? Check the calendar or contact the club to arrange your visit.

1. Park 2. Find the entrance 3. Come upstairs

01 / Park

Start with the marked parking areas.

Use the parking areas marked in teal on the overhead image. The blue arrow points to the club entrance.

After parking, walk toward the wooden railing and staircase shown in the entrance photo below.

  • Teal markings: parking areas
  • ↗Blue arrow: entrance

See the entrance up close ↓

02 / Find the entrance

Look for the wooden railing and green canopy.

The photo shows the entrance to use, with the ramp and wooden staircase leading to the doors beneath the green canopy.

Facing the doors, use the left-hand door for the stairs or the right-hand door for the elevator.

03 / Come upstairs

See you on the third floor.

Taking the stairs

Go through the left-hand door and continue to the top of the stairs. That’s where you’ll find the club, in Unit 3A.

Ramp & elevator access

Use the ramp to reach the entrance, then take the right-hand door for the elevator. Ride to the third floor and turn right as you exit. The club is in Unit 3A.

Need a hand finding us? Call (603) 486-8659. For questions before your visit, email info@snhpinballclub.com.
