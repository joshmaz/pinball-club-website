# SNHPC Game Bringer & Game Rotation technical assessment

## Confirmed product direction

- **Bring a Game** is available to approved website accounts regardless of paid membership status. Offers start private. A Game Coordinator must review and explicitly publish the member-facing version. Publication invites feedback and does not authorize an arrival. Owner identity remains private by default.
- **Game Rotation** is managed by designated Game Coordinators independently of Games Editor permissions. Members with the existing Board designation have full read-only access to offers, lender contacts, private coordinator notes, planned rotations, and rotation, loan, and placement history. Game Coordinators and Website Administrators may confirm actual physical arrivals and departures. Historical corrections are restricted to Games Admins and Website Administrators.
- Each physical cabinet has a persistent identity from the first release. Loan history and location history are separate. Rotation supports club-owned cabinets, open-ended and approximate loans, and optional dates. Planned dates and follow-up reminders never cause automatic departures.

The repository findings below describe staging at commit `707bec5faeff7d60a4e553b636fc42b71cddd46a`. Hosted schema parity, live data, and runtime configuration remain unverified. [Reviewed staging commit](https://github.com/joshmaz/pinball-club-website/commit/707bec5faeff7d60a4e553b636fc42b71cddd46a)

## Proposed data model and workflow

| Record | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Catalog game** | Reuse `games` for title and edition metadata, public slug and images, and existing catalog references. Keep its UUID stable through swaps. OPDB IDs identify editions or aliases and may be shared by multiple cabinets. |
| **Cabinet** | Give each verified physical unit a private, stable UUID linked to its catalog game, with a coordinator-visible label and an optional current owner reference to `owner_parties`. Serial numbers and external IDs are optional reconciliation evidence. Preserve the cabinet ID through return, re-loan, venue transfer, or ownership change. Resolve ownership through the existing party directory. Treat the legacy game-level party link as legacy catalog attribution during rollout and review its eventual migration or retirement with existing consumers. |
| **Offer and publication** | One submission describes one cabinet. Record the submitting member separately from the prospective lender. Capture condition, availability, approximate or open-ended loan terms, transport considerations, catalog match, exact OPDB ID when known, and fallback title. An offer may be unmatched at intake. A coordinator resolves or creates its canonical cabinet before confirming an arrival. Store a reviewed member-facing summary and publication metadata separately from private intake fields. Keep raw free text, contact information, internal notes, and cabinet identifiers out of member browse responses. Require fresh review for publication of edited material. Withdrawal immediately removes the candidate from browse. |
| **Offer interest** | One row per offer and member, with a unique pair. Make “I’d play this!” reversible and safe to retry. Require an eligible account and a published candidate for new interest. Keep voter identities private by default. |
| **Loan** | One private record for each lending episode, linking the cabinet and lender party at the time of the agreement. Store open-ended or approximate terms, start/end information when known, return request and follow-up state, plus a private coordination note. A returning cabinet can have a new loan. A venue move can occur within a single loan. Club-owned cabinets have placement history and require no fictitious loan. A lender for a loan may differ from the current owner of the cabinet. |
| **Location stint** | Extend the existing `game_location_stints` path so a verified stint can identify its cabinet and, where applicable, its loan. Preserve game references and existing public history contracts. A historical or imported stint may remain linked only to the catalog game until a coordinator verifies its cabinet. Track observed arrivals and departures separately from loan terms. |
| **Rotation plan** | Allow arrival-only, departure-only, and paired plans. Identify the incoming offer or cabinet and the specific outgoing cabinet and stint. Store optional planned timing, private notes, and independent confirmation for each side. Preserve confirmations when the other side is canceled or rescheduled. |

Use one editable private note per relevant record initially. Add a note history only if coordinators need multiple authored entries. Derive **Needs Attention** from unreviewed offers, return requests, unresolved identity or Pinball Map observations, incomplete moves, and optional manual follow-up. Dates are reminders and planning context.

### Cabinet identity and Pinball Map reconciliation

1. Assign cabinet IDs to verified physical units. Reuse a cabinet on subsequent offers and loans when its identity is confirmed. Keep two same-title units under one catalog record with distinct cabinet IDs and stints. Link historical records or assign ownership only when supported by club records or coordinator confirmation. Preserve unresolved records as title-level history. Avoid bulk-generating a cabinet per legacy title, slug, lender, or stint.
2. Retain Pinball Map observations as evidence at the granularity the feed supports. The current merge groups by name and its downstream lookup can fall back to title. A Pinball Map machine ID alone does not establish a stable physical identity. Associate imported observations to a cabinet only through a verified mapping with clear venue and lifecycle scope. Queue ambiguous adds/removes, repeat observations, and same-title swaps for review. Replays should be idempotent and must not overwrite confirmed cabinet assignments, dates, or loan history.
3. Keep public current-title behavior compatible. When cabinet tracking is activated for a title, aggregate verified placements so that removing one cabinet does not remove a title with another confirmed cabinet present. Use the existing Pinball Map/manual behavior for legacy titles until they are reconciled. Define the handoff explicitly in the floor-resolution logic and surface conflicting or stale overrides for review. Preserve existing public snapshots, IDs, slugs, links, scores, Pingolf targets, images, and issue associations. Cross-check current and historical lineup views when an imported title-level observation overlaps a verified stint.

### Arrival and departure confirmation

Give Game Coordinators and Website Administrators a narrow rotation capability to confirm physical moves. Allow only Games Admins and Website Administrators to correct confirmed history. Use a separate audited correction operation, with a reason and an explicit link to the corrected record. Apply the same protection to confirmed records in legacy stint writers and import reconciliation. Keep general catalog creation and metadata changes with Games Editors or Website Administrators. If a title is missing, offer review can proceed using a reference match or manual title, with a catalog handoff before arrival confirmation.

Confirm each side only after the physical move. Record the confirmation time and actor separately from the observed move date, which can be unknown. Validate the incoming cabinet and applicable loan, or the exact outgoing open stint. An arrival opens or links the correct stint and, for a lender episode, starts or links the loan. A departure closes the specific stint. End the loan only when return or final handoff is explicitly confirmed. An inter-venue move can close one stint and open another under the same loan. A recorded departure with unknown date needs an explicit closed state or equivalent so a null date alone cannot make the placement appear active.

Use one transaction per confirmation, with a stable operation key, locking or equivalent uniqueness guarantees, and stale-state checks on the plan and cabinet. Reject a conflicting second placement or loan as appropriate. Repeating the same confirmation returns the original outcome. Audit actual moves and authorized corrections with record IDs, actor, and state changes. When a reconciliation or retry cannot determine the correct cabinet, leave the move pending for review.

## Proposed permissions

Permissions are additive across separately held responsibilities. Approved-account eligibility is enforced server-side without reference to dues or membership tier.

| Capability | Approved account | Games Editor only | Game Coordinator | Board designation only | Website Administrator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Submit, see, edit, or withdraw own offers | Own | Own | Own | Own | Own |
| Browse published candidates and toggle own interest | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Read all offers, lender contacts, private notes, plans, and history | — | — | Yes | Full read-only | Yes |
| Review, publish, unpublish, and manage rotations | — | — | Yes | — | Yes |
| Confirm actual moves | — | — | Yes | — | Yes |
| Correct confirmed history | — | Games Admin only | Via separate Games Admin role | Via separate Games Admin role | Yes |
| Edit catalog metadata and use general Games Editor tools | — | Existing access | Via separate Games role | Via separate Games role | Existing access |
| Grant or revoke Game Coordinator | — | — | — | — | Yes, plus Membership Admin if approved |
| Read full Operations messaging and administrative audit history | — | — | — | — | Existing access |

Anonymous users have no offer, interest, or rotation access. Use private base records and purpose-specific authenticated reads and writes for **My offers**, **Published candidates**, and **Game Rotation**. Derive caller identity from the authenticated session. Enforce ownership, capabilities, allowed fields, and state transitions server-side. The Board designation grants the complete oversight read surface, including rotation history. Staff designation alone grants no such access. Games Editor access to the existing party directory remains a separate capability.

Add a canonical `game_coordinator` role to the persisted allowlist, effective-role checks, member role presentation, and explicit grant/revoke rules. Use current server-side membership in the role or current Board designation for protected operations. Use the existing verified-email check for new role grants and existing administrator safeguards. Recommend that Website Administrators and Membership Admins administer this volunteer assignment, consistent with other domain roles, subject to approval. Canonical assignments also derive `website_volunteer`, including existing shared Club Issues access. Retain that behavior unless the club requests an exception. ## Account deletion and retention

The existing deletion workflow removes the Auth account, profile, memberships, and roles. It preserves shared club records with cleared actor references, redacts stored signup mail about the member, and cancels relevant queued signup copies. The documented audit retention period is 12 months. The new feature needs its own lifecycle rules for offer content, lender contacts, and notification payloads. [Account deletion and membership trends](https://github.com/joshmaz/pinball-club-website/blob/707bec5faeff7d60a4e553b636fc42b71cddd46a/docs/account-deletion-and-membership-trends.md)

- Recommend removing interest tied to the deleted account and taking its member-facing offers offline as part of deletion. Clear account attribution in any retained shared records, including historical confirmation actors. Use nullable actor links where history must survive. Preserve the cabinet, verified placement and loan facts, and completed or partially confirmed moves as shared club history. Route actionable open plans to coordinators for follow-up.
- Keep the account identity separate from the lender party. One party can represent an external lender or relate to multiple cabinets and loans. Account deletion should not cascade-delete that party or break an active return. Confirm retention and redaction rules for standalone lender contact details, draft or withdrawn offers, and identifying text in coordinator notes. Minimize copied contact values and keep free text out of audit payloads.
- Integrate feature cleanup with the existing deletion transaction and queue safeguards. Cancel pending feature mail that no longer has a valid recipient or purpose and redact identifying data in retained payloads as policy requires. Consider in-flight delivery explicitly and keep emails to generic portal links with minimal identifying detail. A retry must converge to the same state. Apply existing admin-only messaging and audit access even though Board has full rotation oversight.

## Proposed UI

- **Bring a Game** is a standalone authenticated page linked from the member dashboard. It has **Offer a game**, **My offers**, and **Published candidates** sections. The form searches the catalog first, then OPDB, with manual entry available. My offers shows whether an offer is under review, published for feedback, being coordinated, or closed. Candidate cards carry the approved summary and “I’d play this!” control. Use “Published for member feedback” to distinguish that state from a planned arrival.
- **Game Rotation** is a separate role-gated Member Tools entry. Lead with **Needs Attention**, then **Offers**, **Current lineup**, and **Planned moves**. Offer detail shows a preview of what members will see alongside the private review fields. Current lineup shows one row per confirmed cabinet, with links to the existing game record and private loan and location history. Show the cabinet label, lender or club ownership, rotation openness, return requests, and any unresolved Pinball Map observation. Board users see the same records and history with view-only controls. Planned moves accepts an arrival, a departure, or a pairing, with separate actual confirmation for each move and a preview of the affected cabinet, loan, stint, and public lineup.
- Reuse shared session handling, styles, form/status patterns, and validated portal deep links. Keep rotation UI in a dedicated module. The existing Games panel remains the entry point for catalog edits during the initial handoff workflow. ## Remaining policy decisions

1. **Account approval.** Which existing server-side state represents an approved website account? If the site has no distinct approval state, confirm whether verified email and an active website account suffice or whether approval requires staff action. Paid membership is irrelevant to this decision.
2. **Coordinator delegation.** May Membership Admin grant and revoke Game Coordinator, or is delegation Website Administrator-only?
3. **Deletion and retention.** Approve retention windows and redaction rules for unpublished, withdrawn, and closed offers, contact records, private notes, and stored messages. Specify when to preserve contact details for an active loan and who handles removal requests or a pending return after account deletion.
4. **Publication and signals.** Confirm allowed candidate fields, whether lenders can opt into attribution, whether any consent carries to public catalog attribution, who can see interest counts, and whether a published candidate stays visible with the last reviewed text while a material edit awaits review.
5. **Intake and follow-up.** May coordinators record offers for external lenders? May lenders request returns through the portal? Which status events merit email, and should pending coordinator notifications be suppressed after a role revocation?

The model is ready for implementation scoping with persistent cabinets, independent loans, and explicit confirmation as its foundation. Resolve the approval predicate, role delegation, and deletion rules before finalizing member-facing authorization and data lifecycle. The cabinet and loan foundation can proceed independently.
