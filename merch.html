We love discovering pinball spots around New England and wearing our club pride wherever the games take us. Our merch celebrates the wider pinball community and our own little corner of it: Southern New Hampshire Pinball Club. It gives members a way to carry home a reminder of the games, friends, and excitement they find here.

Merch is fun to wear, and it also helps keep the pinball spirit alive at SNHPC. Proceeds from these sales go directly toward supporting club maintenance and expansion.

Our online shop is where we’ll keep the latest designs and options. The catalog may change as we explore the new platform, so check Fourthwall for current colors, sizing, pricing, and availability.
