Your Support Makes a Difference

Keeping a pinball club running takes more than quarters and extra balls. Behind every game are dedicated volunteers, regular maintenance, and the resources to keep the fun going.

01

Keep the Games Playing

Replacement parts, repair supplies, and routine care keep our machines — especially the classics — ready for the next player.

02

Bring People Together

Support opportunities for casual play, friendly competition, club activities, and welcoming new people into the hobby.

03

Build for the Future

Help maintain and improve our home, grow what the club can offer, and preserve pinball for future generations.

Every Contribution Counts

Whether you are a longtime member, an occasional visitor, or someone who believes in keeping pinball alive, your generosity matters. Give whatever amount feels right for you.

  • $25Small repair parts and supplies

  • MonthlyDependable support for ongoing expenses and future projects

A Club Built by Its Community

SNHPC is powered by people who give their time, skills, and enthusiasm to make the club possible. Financial gifts are one way to help. Volunteering, sharing your knowledge, introducing someone new to pinball, and spreading the word all make a difference.

Every contribution helps make sure the next person through our doors can discover the same excitement that brought us together.
