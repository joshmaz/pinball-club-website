Member sign-in

Your club account brings your profile, membership details, and member access together in one place.

New to the site? Create a free account below, confirm your email, and sign in. A website account is separate from paid Full Access membership.

Welcome to the club

Already have an account? Enter your email and password and choose Login. New here? Enter an email and password, choose Sign Up, and follow the email confirmation link before signing in.

What you can do when you sign in

Keep your profile up to date, connect optional IFPA and Match Play player IDs, check your membership status, and manage your password. Full Access Members can also securely view the club door code. Volunteers with assigned website roles can access the tools they need to help manage games, events, photos, and club operations.

Everyone can browse the public games library, events, and resources without signing in.

Free account or Full Access membership?

Creating a website account is free. New accounts start with Basic Member access. Full Access membership is a separate paid club membership that includes 24/7 access to the pinball room, with games on free play.

Full Access memberships are currently coordinated through the club, not purchased or renewed through this website. For details or to join, contact the club.

Questions or want to help?

SNHPC is a nonprofit, volunteer-run community. If you need account help or would like to get involved, get in touch or talk with a club volunteer at an event.
